# Bike Sales Analysis Dashboard | Tableau


## Overview
Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard using the Europe Bike Store Sales dataset from Kaggle to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer demographics, and product trends across multiple European countries. The dashboard transforms 113,036 sales records into interactive business insights using KPIs, charts, maps, and filters, enabling users to explore revenue, profit, customer behavior, and product performance from 2011–2016.


## Tools & Technologies
Tableau | Microsoft Excel | Kaggle Dataset 


## Dataset Information
- ### Attribute              - Details
- Dataset                     Europe Bike Store Sales (Kaggle)
- Total Records               113,036
- Total Columns               18
- Years Covered               2011–2016 (6 Years)
- Countries                   6
- Product Categories          3 (Bikes, Accessories, Clothing)

## Dashboard Features
### Executive KPIs
- Revenue
- Profit
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Profit Growth
### Interactive Analysis
- Top 10 Products Sold by Quantity
- Product Category Sales by Gender
- Sales by Customer Age Group
- Country-wise Sales Analysis
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue Trend Analysis
### Interactive Filters
- Year Filter (2011–2016)
- Country Filter (6 Countries)


## Key Insights
- Analyzed 113,036 sales transactions across 6 years.
- Tracked business performance using 3 executive KPIs.
- Identified the Top 10 highest-selling products by quantity.
- Compared purchasing trends across 4 customer age groups.
- Analyzed gender-wise sales contribution across product categories.
- Visualized sales distribution across 6 countries using an interactive map.
- Evaluated revenue generated from 3 major product categories.
- Enabled fully interactive dashboard filtering for faster business exploration.
